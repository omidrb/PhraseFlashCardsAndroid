Privacy Policy — Zope Flash Cards

Developer: Zope Lab
Effective date: 6 October 2026

Zope Lab built Zope Flash Cards as a flash-card and language-learning application. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data collection and sharing

Zope Flash Cards does not require an account and does not send your flash-card content, native-language choice, practice results, favorites, tags, or app preferences to Zope Lab. The app does not include advertising or analytics services in this release, and Zope Lab does not sell user data.

Information stored on your device

The app stores flash cards and settings locally on your Android device. This can include phrases, meanings, examples, tags, favorites, Leitner/practice progress, language selections, voice settings, notification settings, and other app preferences.

CSV import/export and backup

If you choose to import or export CSV files or create/restore a backup, Android's system file picker is used so you can select the file or destination. The selected data is processed for the requested import, export, backup, or restore operation. Zope Lab does not receive these files.

Text-to-speech

Zope Flash Cards uses Android text-to-speech functionality to read flash-card text aloud. Text-to-speech processing is provided by the TTS engine configured on your device. The behavior and data practices of that engine are governed by its provider.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app uses Android notification and scheduling features to display local flash-card practice notifications. Notification preferences are stored on your device. The app also listens for device restart so enabled reminders can be scheduled again.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission on Android versions that require it and uses the device boot-completed event to restore scheduled reminders. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or phone permissions.

Data retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it using app functions, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported CSV and backup files remain wherever you chose to save them and can be deleted using your device's file-management tools.

Children

Zope Flash Cards is a general-purpose learning utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not transmit user-created flash-card data to Zope Lab.

Security

Because the app's flash-card data is stored locally, protection of that data also depends on the security of your Android device and any locations where you choose to save exported or backup files.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions about Zope Flash Cards, contact Zope Lab through the project's GitHub repository: Zope Flash Cards GitHub Issues.
